Parts to fit after assembly
Measure the installed boxes before cutting finish parts, hardware openings or the wall support.


01 Poplar face trim
Use 1/4" thickness for the slim front we discussed. Fit two continuous horizontal rails across the 93" run.
Cut three seam covers and two end strips to fit between the rails. Seam covers are about 1 1/2 wide.
Match each strip to the plywood edges so the trim sits flush with the bay openings. Size rails to cover the edges.



02 Shelves and inset door
Fit the shelf blanks to the assembled openings. Target about 18" depth where the hardware and cables permit.
Fit one flat inset door in D. With a 1/8" reveal on every edge, subtract 1/4" from the measured width and height.
Verify that reveal with the chosen hinges. Test the 35 mm hinge cups and plate setback on scrap with the trim.
For a knob or pull and self-closing hinges, set the adjustable stop after aligning the door flush with the trim.



03 Stainable butcher-block cap
One continuous cap spans the 93" cabinet and 19" subwoofer alcove. Start with one 10 ft blank.
Target 112" wide x about 20 1/4" deep x 1 1/2" thick. Fit the depth to the installed trim and wall.
Set the alcove support flush with the plywood tops so it supports the cap independently of the subwoofer.
Account for the slab weight in the support design. Follow the slab maker's fastening and expansion instructions.



04 Backs, surround and wall support
Keep the Denon rear open for outlets, plugs and airflow. Size any removable liners and storage back after test fit.
Keep the subwoofer floor-supported. Measure it with cords attached before cutting its separate surround.
See pages 5-8 for the ledger, box assembly, mounting strips and direct fastening sequence.

Mount and join the cabinet boxes
Direct fastening through the rear strips into wood studs and secured in-wall blocking



   LAY OUT PERMANENT FASTENERS BEFORE PATCHING THE WALL
   Use the wall positions on page 3 and the 8 1/2" cabinet-bottom line. Transfer the actual rear strip locations.
   Provide spaced upper and lower fastening points for each box. Add blocking where those points miss studs.
   Verify blocking attachment, strip joints, and wall fasteners for the combined box, cap and equipment load.



01 Open and document the wall
Check for wiring and shut off the affected circuit before opening the wall near the outlet group.
Secure the blocking to the framing. Record the stud centers and blocking limits from the left wall
and the same height reference, photograph them with a tape in view, then patch the drywall.


02 Set each box on temporary supports
Use the rear ledger and front platform on page 5. Set an empty box in place with a helper.
Align it to the elevation, check level and plumb, and shim at the anchorage points as needed.


03 Fasten and align
Fasten through the upper and lower strips into verified studs or blocking with specified cabinet anchorage screws.
Check the next box against the first. Clamp adjacent sides so the faces sit flush and join them with cabinet
connector screws sized for the two side panels. Verify that the screw tips stay inside the paired panels.


04 Remove temporary support
Confirm the permanent attachments in every box before removing the temporary ledger and platform.
Patch the ledger screw holes. Keep all equipment and the slab off the boxes until the supports are ready.

Fit the shelves, trim and inset door
Finish the visible parts after the boxes are aligned and the actual openings are known


01 Dry-fit equipment and shelving
Place the receiver and consoles in their bays with cables attached. Check rear clearance and ventilation.
Trim the two shelf blanks to the finished openings. Allow clearance for shelf hardware and door swing.
Use about 18" shelf depth where the hardware, airflow and cables permit. Fit storage backs or removable
liners only after the wall attachments are checked. Keep Denon plugs and fasteners accessible.


02 Fit the poplar face trim
Cut two continuous horizontal rails from 8 ft stock. Fit the three vertical seam covers and two end strips
between them. The trim is 1/4" thick. Size each piece to cover the plywood edges and preserve bay openings.
Dry-fit the rails and stiles with the boxes clamped flush. Glue to clean wood, clamp or pin, then fill and sand.


03 Build and hang the right-hand inset door
Cut a flat 3/4" paint-grade plywood door to the measured opening minus the chosen reveals.
A 1/8" reveal per edge subtracts 1/4" from both width and height. Check the hinge template first.
Test a 35 mm cup and inset plate on scrap with the trim in place. Use matching self-closing inset hinges
and plates for the knob or pull option. Hang and adjust the door, then fit the stop on the side opposite the hinges.


04 Prep and paint
Fill plywood edges and visible fastener holes, sand, prime, then apply warm white cabinet paint.
Paint and let parts cure before fitting lights, door hardware and electronics. Keep glue surfaces bare
until the trim has been attached. Protect the painted face while fitting the top.


   ORDER OF FIT
   Align boxes, fit shelves and trim, measure the door, then finish. Keep the door removable for hinge adjustment.

Support the alcove and fit the cap
One 112-inch butcher-block cap | Finish the alcove and cabinets before taking the final slab measurements


01 Build the separate alcove support
Lay out the 19" left zone from page 3. Test-fit the floor-standing subwoofer with plugs and cables attached.
Use the field-fit surround detail on page 11. Measure the actual unit and allow cable and vent clearance.
Build the surround so its load-bearing top is in the same plane as the cabinet tops, about 24 1/2" above carpet.
Verify the surround carries its share of slab weight independently. Leave clearance around the subwoofer.


02 Fit and fasten the butcher block
Start with the 10 ft blank. Dry-fit the 112" length and scribe the depth to the wall and finished face trim.
Check continuous seating and the required wall clearance. Follow the slab maker's directions for acclimation,
finishing, support and movement. Use the specified movement-tolerant fastening detail from below.
Confirm the cap seats across the transition from floating cabinets to alcove support before fastening.


03 Fit lighting and route cables
Dry-fit the warm LED channels inside the open bays and below the floating run. Test both zones first.
Keep the driver and controller accessible. Route cords and low-voltage cables through smooth openings,
away from hinge travel, cabinet screws and hot vents. Follow the lighting maker's wiring instructions.


04 Load and check
Check that the boxes are secure, the face seams are flush, and the door moves freely.
Set the equipment in place, verify plug access and airflow, and test the lighting and speakers.
Make the final fit check with the subwoofer standing on the carpet, clear of the surround.
